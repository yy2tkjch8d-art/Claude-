The house

Built into the rock, not onto it.

The cliff formed from a lava flow roughly 12,000 years ago. We kept its face intact. Suites are carved straight back into the stone, and from the sea all you see at night is a scatter of warm windows.

Inside, it is quiet. The walls are 1.8 metres of solid rock, so rooms hold an even 21°C all year with almost no air conditioning. There are no televisions and no lobby music. Staff are on hand day and night but stay out of sight.

Thermal baths

Fire, then the Atlantic.

Three springs rise through the basalt beneath the hotel. Bathe in this order, slowly. A full circuit takes about 50 minutes.

  1. STEP 138°C

    Iron pool. Rust-red, mineral-rich water in a cave lit from below. 15 minutes.

  2. STEP 241°C

    Steam vault. Natural steam vented from the rock. Eight minutes at most.

  3. STEP 312°C

    Sea plunge. A tidal rock pool refilled by every wave. As long as you can stand.

  4. STEP 434°C

    Rest pool. Open to the sky, at body temperature. Stay until you forget the time.

The table · 14 seats

Cooked in the ground.

Each morning, pots of meat and roots are lowered into a geothermal vent behind the kitchen. They come up six hours later, just before sunset. Everything else is caught or grown within sight of the dining room.

One tasting menu a night, served at 19:40. That is the time of the average sunset here over the year.

  1. I

    Limpets, seawater butter, lava salt

    From the rocks under the sea plunge.

  2. II

    Blue mackerel cured over fig leaf

    Line-caught that afternoon.

  3. III

    Volcano-stewed beef, 6 hours in the earth

    Island cattle, kale, sweet potato.

  4. IV

    Passion fruit, sheep's curd, pink pepper

    Grown on the terraces below the cliff.
