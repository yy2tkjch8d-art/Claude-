Terms and privacy

Terms of sale

  • Every ticket admits one person aged 21 or older. Bring your original Emirates ID, passport or GCC ID. Photos or copies are not accepted.
  • The name on the order must match the ID of the person attending, unless you moved the ticket to someone else through us.
  • Right of admission is reserved. We can refuse entry or remove anyone who breaks venue rules, UAE law or these terms, with no refund.
  • Lineups can change. A change of artist is not a reason for a refund.
  • We photograph and film our events for our socials. If you don’t want to appear, tell the door staff and we’ll keep you out of shots.
  • A VIP table covers the guests and package listed when you bought it. Extra guests or bottles are paid on the night.

Refunds and transfers

  • Tickets are non-refundable once bought.
  • You can move a ticket to a friend up to 24 hours before doors. Message us with the order email and the new name.
  • If we cancel or postpone a night, you can get a full refund to the card you paid with. Refunds take 5 to 10 working days to reach your card.
  • No refunds for entry refused because of age, ID, dress code or behaviour.

Privacy

We collect your name, email, WhatsApp number and order details when you buy a ticket or join a guest list. We use them to send your ticket and location, run the door list and tell you about our events if you agree to it.

  • Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Guest list requests go through WhatsApp, which Meta operates under its own privacy policy.
  • We keep order records for as long as UAE accounting rules require, and contact details until you ask us to remove them.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time through the contact details on our home page.
